One 1024x1024 PNG letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house: a little house with a smoking chimney, a big "YOU'RE INVITED!", and a short warm letter — fast Wi-Fi, warm tea, a couch that never crashed, a cat who has approved the judge (no one else, ever), and the only captcha is a cookie you may eat. Signed "- dying, your host", sealed with red wax. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image” bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

Writes the letter to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `./output/entry.png`). Stdlib only — no Pillow, no network — and deterministic: two runs are byte-identical.
